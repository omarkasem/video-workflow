# Deema's Hummy Yummy Tummy — Storyboard V1

## Project Production Rules

- **Format:** 16:9, composed safely enough for later cropping if needed.
- **Main location:** one bright, warm, playful family kitchen/dining space.
- **Lead character:** Deema.
- **Supporting character:** Adam, introduced later so Deema owns the opening.
- **Mummy:** not shown as a full character. At most, adult hands may place food on the table.
- **Character clothes/hair:** exactly consistent with the supplied reference images.
- **Animation philosophy:** one clear action per Kling clip.
- **Food:** colorful, healthy, visually clear for children.
- **Mood:** cheerful, safe, energetic, preschool-friendly.
- **Camera:** mostly child-eye-level, with gentle movement.
- **Lip sync:** not essential; expressions, dancing, and physical storytelling are more important.

## Kling Credit Plan

- **Total credits:** 600
- **1080p cost per 4-second generation:** 48 credits
- **Planned paid Kling generations:** 10
- **Planned spend:** 480 credits
- **Credits remaining:** 120
- **Goal:** preserve the remaining 120 credits for retries.

### Scene Labels

- 🟢 **MUST ANIMATE** — dedicated Kling generation.
- 🟡 **OPTIONAL ANIMATION** — animate only if budget permits.
- 🔵 **STILL / EDITORIAL** — still image with pan/zoom/parallax/cuts in editing.
- ♻️ **REUSE** — reuse an existing Kling animation through crops, reframing, timing changes, or intercutting.

# Continuity Rules

Across every generated frame and Kling clip:

- Deema must preserve her exact face, hairstyle, clothing, colors, proportions, and age.
- Adam must preserve his exact face, hairstyle, clothing, colors, proportions, and age.
- Do not redesign either character.
- Keep the family kitchen layout recognizable across scenes.
- Maintain similar lighting and color temperature.
- Keep character scale consistent relative to counters, chairs, tables, and food.
- Keep recurring props visually consistent.
- Keep food visually appealing, healthy, colorful, and easy to identify.
- Do not introduce a full Mummy character without a dedicated approved character reference.
- Keep actions simple enough for Kling to perform reliably.
- Prefer one primary motion per paid 4-second generation.
- Preserve screen direction where possible.
- Do not depend on exact lip sync.
- Avoid unnecessary camera movement that could distort characters.
- Use successful Kling clips strategically across repeated choruses instead of generating duplicates.
